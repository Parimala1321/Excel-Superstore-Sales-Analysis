# 📊 Superstore Sales Analytics — Excel Project

## 📖 About the Project

An end-to-end Excel analytics project on the classic Superstore retail dataset. The raw order data is cleaned, structured as an Excel Table, summarised with PivotTables, and presented through interactive report sheets and an executive summary covering sales, profit, customers, products, regions and discounts.

## 🎯 Project Objective

- 🧹 Clean and standardise the raw order data
- 🧮 Calculate core business KPIs such as sales, profit, margin and order value
- 🔎 Analyse performance by customer segment, product, region and discount level
- 🚀 Turn the findings into clear recommendations for the business

## 🗃️ Dataset

Superstore sales data with 9,994 order lines, 793 customers and orders from January 2014 to December 2017, across four US regions: Central, East, South and West.

## 📒 Workbook Analysis

- 📥 **Raw_Data** – original Superstore export
- 🧹 **Data_Quality_Report** – issues found in the data and the decisions taken
- ✨ **Clean_Data** – cleaned dataset with standardised dates and a discount band column
- 🔍 **Lookup_Centre** – enter an Order ID to get customer, product, category, location and quantity details
- 🧮 **KPIS_Calculations** – core KPI formulas
- 👥 **Report_CustomerAnalysis** – segment performance and top customers by profit
- 📦 **Report_ProductAnalysis** – category and sub-category performance, top and bottom products
- 🗺️ **Report_RegionalAnalysis** – region and category profit matrix with state-level performance
- 🏷️ **Report_DiscountAnalysis** – category-wise discount and discount band versus profit
- 📝 **Summary_Report** – executive summary with key findings and recommendations

## 📋 Reports

1. 👥 **Customer Analysis** – compares Consumer, Corporate and Home Office segments on sales, discount, profit and margin, and lists the top 10 customers by profit.
2. 📦 **Product Analysis** – breaks down categories into sub-categories with sales, profit, quantity and margin, plus the top 5 and bottom 5 products by profit.
3. 🗺️ **Regional Analysis** – shows profit by region and category, with a state-level view filtered by region.
4. 🏷️ **Discount Analysis** – compares average discount and profit across categories and discount bands.
5. 📝 **Summary Report** – brings the KPIs, key findings and strategic recommendations together on one page.

## 💡 Key Insights

- 💰 Total sales of $2.30M and total profit of $598.7K, at an overall profit margin of about 26%
- 🛍️ The Consumer segment is the revenue leader, contributing about 51% of sales and profit
- ⚖️ All three segments hold a near-identical margin of around 26%
- 💻 Technology is the most profitable category with the lowest average discount (13.2%)
- 🪑 Furniture receives the highest average discount (17.4%) and has the weakest margin among the categories
- 💺 Chairs has the highest sales of any sub-category but only about a 14% margin
- 🏆 Binders has the best margin of any sub-category at about 53%
- 📍 The East region leads in profit ($190.7K), while the South trails at $101.8K
- ✅ Recommendations: cap Furniture discounts, push Technology sales, fix the South region and improve Chairs margin
